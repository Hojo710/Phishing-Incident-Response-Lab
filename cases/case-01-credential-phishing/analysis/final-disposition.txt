CASE 01 - CREDENTIAL PHISHING
FINAL DISPOSITION

Disposition:
Malicious - Credential Phishing Attempt

Severity:
Low

User Interaction:
No interaction reported.

Compromise Status:
No evidence of credential theft, account compromise, or endpoint compromise was observed.

Disposition Rationale:
The email was assessed as a malicious credential-phishing attempt based on multiple correlated indicators. The message impersonated Microsoft 365, used suspicious sender and Reply-To domains, failed SPF and DMARC authentication checks, had no DKIM authentication result, contained a credential-verification link on a separate suspicious domain, and used urgency and the threat of account suspension to encourage user interaction.

The recipient reported that they did not click the credential-verification link or submit credentials. Therefore, the investigation found no evidence that the intended credential-capture stage of the attack was successful.

Severity Rationale:
The incident was assigned Low severity under the severity model used for this lab. Although the email was assessed as malicious, no successful user interaction or subsequent compromise was observed. Severity classifications may differ between organizations depending on their internal incident-response procedures and risk criteria.

MITRE ATT&CK Summary:
- T1566.002 - Phishing: Spearphishing Link - Observed
- T1204.001 - User Execution: Malicious Link - Intended / Not Observed
- T1056.003 - Input Capture: Web Portal Capture - Intended / Not Observed

Final Analyst Assessment:
The available evidence supports classifying the message as a malicious credential-phishing attempt. The attack reached the initial delivery stage, but the intended user-execution and credential-capture stages were not observed. No evidence currently supports escalation to a confirmed account or endpoint compromise.
