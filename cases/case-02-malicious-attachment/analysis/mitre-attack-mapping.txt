CASE 02 - MALICIOUS ATTACHMENT
MITRE ATT&CK MAPPING

Case:
Simulated payroll-themed phishing email containing:
Employee_Salary_Adjustments_2026.pdf.exe


1. T1566.001 - Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment

Tactic:
Initial Access

Evidence Status:
Observed

Evidence:
The simulated phishing email delivered the attachment
Employee_Salary_Adjustments_2026.pdf.exe to the recipient.

Analysis:
The attachment was delivered through a phishing email that used payroll and salary-adjustment themes to encourage the recipient to open the file.


2. T1204.002 - User Execution: Malicious File

Tactic:
Execution

Evidence Status:
Intended / Not Observed on Employee Endpoint

Evidence:
The phishing email instructed the employee to open the attached file and review salary information. The employee reported that the attachment was not opened.

Analysis:
The social-engineering attempt was designed to persuade the recipient to execute the malicious attachment. However, no evidence establishes that the employee opened or executed the file.


3. T1059.001 - Command and Scripting Interpreter: PowerShell

Tactic:
Execution

Evidence Status:
Observed in Simulated Sandbox Only

Evidence:
During simulated sandbox execution,
Employee_Salary_Adjustments_2026.pdf.exe launched powershell.exe.

Analysis:
PowerShell execution was directly observed in the simulated sandbox environment. This behavior was not observed on the employee endpoint and should not be attributed to the employee's system.


4. T1105 - Ingress Tool Transfer

Tactic:
Command and Control

Evidence Status:
Potentially Supported / Requires Additional Evidence

Evidence:
The simulated sandbox showed powershell.exe communicating with
update-payroll.example. A secondary executable,
C:\Users\Public\payroll-update.exe, was subsequently created and executed.

Analysis:
The sequence is consistent with a possible second-stage payload transfer. However, the available simulated evidence does not directly establish that payroll-update.exe was transferred from update-payroll.example. Additional network or sandbox evidence would be required to make that claim.


EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The phishing email provides evidence of attachment delivery to the recipient.

The simulated sandbox provides evidence of behavior that occurred when the simulated attachment was executed in that separate environment.

Sandbox behavior does not establish that the same execution occurred on the employee endpoint.

No employee endpoint compromise is established by the available evidence.
