IMPACT ASSESSMENT

The employee received a phishing email that used Microsoft 365 impersonation and urgency-based social engineering to direct the employee to a credential-harvesting website. The employee reported entering and submitting their work credentials to the site.

Further investigation identified a successful unauthorized authentication associated with source IP 198.51.100.219. Post-authentication activity from the same source included mailbox access, creation of an inbox rule, a mailbox search for "invoice," and access to the employee's OneDrive data.

Audit evidence established that three files were successfully downloaded during the unauthorized session:

- Q3-Financial-Forecast.xlsx
- Vendor-Payment-Details.xlsx
- Department-Budget-2027.xlsx

Content review determined that the affected files contained organizational financial information. Two files were classified as Confidential, while Vendor-Payment-Details.xlsx was classified as Restricted and contained vendor names, payment amounts, invoice numbers, and bank-routing information.

The successful downloads during the unauthorized session support confirmed data exfiltration of these three files within the simulated incident.

SEVERITY ASSESSMENT

Severity:
High

Rationale:
The incident progressed beyond phishing and credential exposure to successful unauthorized account access and post-authentication activity. The unauthorized session successfully downloaded three files containing Confidential and Restricted organizational financial information.

The combination of confirmed account compromise, unauthorized mailbox and OneDrive activity, and confirmed exfiltration of sensitive organizational information supports a High severity classification under the lab's incident-severity model.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The available evidence establishes exfiltration of the three identified files. It does not establish that additional files or information were exfiltrated beyond the activity identified during the reviewed session.

The scope should be expanded if additional download, transfer, or unauthorized account activity is identified.
