CASE 03 - EMPLOYEE STATEMENT

Employee:
employee@company.example

Reported Interaction:

The employee reported receiving an unexpected Microsoft 365 security notification.

The employee stated:

"I clicked the Review Recent Activity link because I thought the email was from Microsoft. The page looked like a Microsoft 365 login page. I entered my work email address and password and clicked Sign In. The page displayed an error afterward, so I closed it. I did not download or open any files."


ANALYST OBSERVATIONS

Based on the employee's statement, the following actions are reported:

1. The employee clicked the link contained in the suspicious email.

2. The employee reached a webpage presented as a Microsoft 365 login page.

3. The employee entered their work email address and password.

4. The employee submitted those credentials by selecting Sign In.

5. The employee reported that the webpage displayed an error afterward.

6. The employee reported that no files were downloaded or opened.


EVIDENCE ASSESSMENT

The employee statement establishes reported user interaction with the suspicious website and reported submission of work credentials.

The submitted credentials should be treated as exposed.

The employee statement alone does not establish that an unauthorized individual successfully authenticated to the employee's Microsoft 365 account or that additional account activity occurred.

Authentication and account-activity evidence must be reviewed separately to determine whether the exposed credentials were subsequently used.


EVIDENCE SOURCE

The findings documented above are based on the employee's reported actions. They should be distinguished from independently observed authentication, endpoint, or network telemetry.
