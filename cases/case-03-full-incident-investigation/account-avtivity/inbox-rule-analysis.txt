ANALYST ASSESSMENT

The inbox rule was created during the confirmed unauthorized session from
198.51.100.219.

The rule was configured to identify messages containing security-related
terms, mark those messages as read, and move them to Deleted Items. This
functionality would reduce the likelihood that the employee would notice
security notifications associated with account activity and is consistent
with an attempt to conceal unauthorized activity.

The rule contained no forwarding address. Therefore, this rule was not
configured to forward email messages to an external account. This does not
rule out other possible methods of email access or exfiltration.

RESPONSE ACTION

Preserve the inbox rule configuration as evidence, then disable or remove
the unauthorized rule as part of account containment.
