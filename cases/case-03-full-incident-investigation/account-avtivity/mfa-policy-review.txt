CASE 03 - MFA / CONDITIONAL ACCESS POLICY REVIEW

User:
employee@company.example


MFA REGISTRATION

Registered Method:
Microsoft Authenticator

Status:
Registered


EXPECTED ACCESS POLICY

Normal Sign-In Policy:
MFA is required for external sign-ins.

Expected Behavior:
An external authentication attempt should be evaluated by the applicable Conditional Access policy and require MFA when the policy conditions are met.


08:37 AUTHENTICATION REVIEW

Source IP:
198.51.100.219

Device:
Unknown / New Device

MFA Result:
Not Satisfied

MFA Challenge Issued:
No

Conditional Access Policy Applied:
No


POLICY REVIEW FINDING

The employee account was unintentionally excluded from the organization's Conditional Access policy requiring MFA for external sign-ins.

As a result, the 08:37 external authentication was not evaluated under the expected MFA requirement, and no MFA challenge was issued.


ANALYST ASSESSMENT

The available evidence does not support describing the authentication as an MFA bypass.

An MFA bypass would imply that an MFA control was applied and subsequently defeated, circumvented, or otherwise overcome.

In this incident, the evidence indicates that the expected Conditional Access policy was not applied because the employee account was excluded from the policy.

The unauthorized authentication therefore succeeded without an MFA challenge being issued.

This represents a security-control configuration gap that contributed to the account compromise.


IMMEDIATE ACCOUNT RESPONSE

1. Reset the employee's Microsoft 365 password because the employee reported submitting the credentials to the phishing site and subsequent evidence supports unauthorized use.

2. Revoke active authentication sessions/tokens associated with the employee account to contain existing authenticated access.

3. Remove the unintended Conditional Access exclusion and ensure the account is covered by the organization's MFA requirement for external sign-ins.

4. Review the corrected policy configuration to verify that the intended MFA requirement is applied.


EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The evidence establishes that no MFA challenge was issued during the anomalous authentication and that the employee account was excluded from the applicable Conditional Access policy.

The evidence does not establish that an attacker defeated an MFA challenge or used a specific MFA-bypass technique.
